● How IGSO Works Under the Hood

1. Grid Neighbors (3ᵈ - 1)

Samples 3ᵈ - 1 surrounding candidate points at offset ±s across all coordinate axes (e.g. 8 in 2D, 80 in 4D).

2. Best Candidate

Identifies neighbor with lowest f(x, y). If lower than current node, node jumps there.

3. Step Adaptation

Expands step (×expand) when Δf > T. Contracts (×shrink) when Δf ≤ T to zero in on minimum.

4. Derivative-Free

Requires no gradients; naturally navigates non-differentiable cusps and sharp corners.
